# Antoine Wodniack
https://wodniack.dev/

## The good
1. Colors: The red and black is clean, but striking.
2. Animation: The little tool at the top is pretty cool, and a lot of the effects on the page look really good.
The whole page really reminds me of the game Valorant and other Riot motion graphics
3. The gifs in the work section being links to the other sites is convenient

## The less good
1. As good as the animations look, having all of them on the same page can really impact the performance; 
Scrolling up or down too fast causes the whole page to lag (at least, on my laptop running it on Chrome)
2. While the animations and colors look great, I think they only work because he's an animator. Flashy effects like these
which limit visual clarity wouldn't be a great fit for a non-artistic field

# Karina Farek
https://www.karinafarek.com/

## The Good
1. Simplicity: This is the portfolio site of one of my favorite digital artists. Since it's meant to show off artwork, 
I think it's better that it doesn't have a flashy UI distracting from the work itself. The art in each page is laid out well.
2. The page navigation. The side panel is on every page, and has links to every other page. There's an animated gif of her
logo on it as well, which helps tie it together.

## The less good
2. As much as I praised the side panel, I do think it's a bit too big. I think the image could be smaller and it could
be moved over slightly, since it currently makes the pages look a bit small.
